Website Checker · Pre-launch review

I use OpenClaw and custom-written skills to help the team perform its final website review before launch. Browser-based checks cover grammar, typos, layout, responsive behaviour, and the accuracy of website information, following the company’s review process and checklist.

Review throughput

Previously, checking one website took 30 minutes. With this workflow, six websites can be checked in 30 minutes, reducing the amount of manual review.

Manage Website · Internal API integration

OpenClaw works with the internal system’s APIs to manage website backends, including uploading products and services. I configure OpenClaw to understand the company’s APIs. Proper handling of API tokens to avoid exposure is a key requirement.

AI Competitor Analysis · Seminar booth

OpenClaw gathers information about a company’s competitors and compares strengths and weaknesses against custom rules and conditions. It generates AI-recommended SEO, GEO, and AEO keywords, then produces a report to send to the customer. This workflow was used at a company seminar booth to help participants.

Google Overview · Review monitoring

OpenClaw collects businesses’ Google reviews and sends alerts for negative feedback. Positive reviews are used to generate reflection-style copy on a schedule, which is automatically published in the business website’s news section.

Leads · Sales research

OpenClaw collects information from the internet, checks each lead against defined rules and conditions, and provides valuable leads to the sales team.

CLIProxyAPI · AI cost optimization

I researched and applied CLIProxyAPI within the company as a relay that makes subscription-based AI services accessible through an API. This approach helps reduce company AI costs and supports higher-volume image generation using those subscriptions. Generation capacity remains subject to the underlying services’ usage limits.

Project focus

The aim of these automations is to address tasks that take too much time, are prone to errors, or involve repetitive work.
